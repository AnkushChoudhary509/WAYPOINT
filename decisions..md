1. Why this approach?
I chose to build Waypoint as a clean, responsive product landing page focused on communicating its
core value within the first few seconds. The design uses a strong hero section, a clear value proposition,
a primary call to action, and a product visualization showing how Waypoint monitors deployments and
can roll them back when something goes wrong.
I kept the interface focused rather than adding unnecessary sections or animations. The product
visualization was chosen to make the concept understandable without relying only on marketing text. I
also avoided fabricated testimonials, fake customer counts, and fake logos to keep the presentation
honest.
2. Trade-off under the time limit
I prioritized the main landing-page experience, responsive design, visual consistency, and the
deployment-monitoring interaction instead of building many additional sections or complex
animations.
With a full week, I would further improve accessibility, add more interactive product states, perform
broader browser and device testing, and optimize performance in more detail.
3. AI usage
I used AI tools as a development assistant for brainstorming, frontend implementation guidance, and
reviewing parts of the code. AI helped accelerate development, but I personally reviewed and modified
the output.
